Settings

The keys and options Audema's agents use. Set your platform defaults here instead of in Vercel. To sell Audema to a client who brings their own (their own Anthropic key, email sender, LinkedIn or Google app…), add them as a client, put their users in it on the Users page, and set the client's own values. Anything a client leaves blank uses your default. Values are stored encrypted and never shown again. Supabase, the cron secret, the encryption key and billing stay in Vercel.
