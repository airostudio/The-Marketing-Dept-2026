Head of Government Funding & Grants

Vera — the Government Funding Room

Not a grant writer. The remit runs funding intelligence → eligibility → strategy → partnerships → applications → acquittals → next opportunity, across federal, Victorian and local government, the R&D Tax Incentive, EMDG, commercialisation programs, research and university partnerships, government tenders and international funding. Every opportunity is scored before anyone writes a word — that is what stops a funding function becoming an application factory.

Funding pipeline
36-month non-dilutive funding target

A target rather than a hope. Secured counts only what has actually been awarded; the pipeline figure is discounted by each opportunity's own probability-of-success score, because an undiscounted pipeline total is the number that makes a funding plan look healthy right up until nothing lands.

How the specialist is engaged

Deliberately not a single large success commission — that pays for submissions, not judgement, and judgement is the thing being bought. A GO / NO-GO score is required before every application.
