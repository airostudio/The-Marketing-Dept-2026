Compliance Centre
Australian and international legal framework for compliant B2B prospecting

🇦🇺 Australian Spam Act 2003

Rules for commercial electronic messages sent to Australian recipients.

  • All emails must identify your business clearly
  • Include a functional unsubscribe mechanism
  • Honour unsubscribes within 5 business days
  • Only send to those who have consented (express or inferred)
  • Inferred consent: prior business relationship or publicly listed contact
  • Do not use address-harvesting software
  • B2B emails to published business addresses are generally permitted

🔒 Privacy Act 1988 (AU)

Governs collection, use and disclosure of personal information in Australia.

  • Only collect information that is reasonably necessary
  • Use only publicly available business directory data
  • Do not collect sensitive personal information without consent
  • Provide a privacy policy accessible to prospects
  • Allow individuals to access and correct their data
  • Secure storage of any collected prospect data
  • Do not sell or share personal data without consent

🇪🇺 GDPR (if targeting EU)

Applies when prospecting European businesses or individuals.

  • Identify your lawful basis (legitimate interest for B2B)
  • Complete a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)
  • Inform prospects of data processing at first contact
  • Provide easy opt-out in every message
  • Respect data subject rights (access, deletion, portability)
  • Do not transfer data outside EU without safeguards
  • Maintain records of processing activities

🇺🇸 CAN-SPAM (if targeting US)

US federal law for commercial email. Lower bar than AU/EU but still requires:

  • Accurate "From" and subject line information
  • Clear identification as commercial message
  • Physical mailing address in every email
  • Opt-out mechanism in every email
  • Process opt-outs within 10 business days
  • Do not sell or transfer opt-out lists
