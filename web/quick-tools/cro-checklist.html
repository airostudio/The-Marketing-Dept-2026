Cart & Checkout

0/8
Guest checkout option available
Don't force account creation. 34% of shoppers abandon carts when forced to create an account.
High ImpactShopify
Express checkout options (Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay)
One-click checkout can increase conversion by 50%+ for returning customers.
High ImpactShopify
Clear shipping costs shown early
Show shipping in cart before checkout. Unexpected costs are #1 reason for cart abandonment.
High Impact
Free shipping threshold displayed
"Add $X more for FREE shipping" - increases AOV by 30% on average.
High ImpactQuick Win
Cart abandonment email sequence set up
Recover 5-15% of abandoned carts with automated email reminders.
High ImpactShopify
Order summary visible throughout checkout
Keep cart contents visible so customers can review their order.
Quick Win
Multiple payment options available
Credit cards, PayPal, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Buy Now Pay Later.
Shopify
Discount code field visible but not prominent
Don't make non-coupon users feel they're missing out. Collapsible field works well.

Page Speed & Performance

0/6
Compressed images (WebP format preferred)
Use TinyPNG or Shopify's built-in compression. WebP saves 25-35% file size.
High ImpactTechnical
Minimal apps installed
Each app adds code. Audit and remove unused apps. Keep under 15 apps.
High ImpactShopify
Lazy loading enabled for images
Images load as user scrolls. Shopify 2.0 themes support this natively.
TechnicalShopify
Core Web Vitals passing
LCP under 2.5s, FID under 100ms, CLS under 0.1. Check in Google Search Console.
High ImpactTechnical
Reduce third-party scripts
Chat widgets, analytics, tracking pixels. Each adds load time.
Technical
Use Shopify's CDN for all assets
Shopify's CDN delivers content from servers closest to your customers.
