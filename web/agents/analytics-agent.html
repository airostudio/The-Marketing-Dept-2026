Enter your budget allocation across channels to get an AI narrative on likely ROI and reallocation ideas. This reasons over the numbers you enter — it doesn't run a statistical marketing mix model (regression, saturation curves, adstock decay) the way a tool like Meridian or Robyn does.

✓ Real calculation Budget Reallocation Simulator
Drag any channel's budget and every number below recalculates instantly — deterministic math on the figures you entered above, not an AI guess.

*Sums conversions/leads and revenue-derived figures together when your channels use different units — trust the per-channel % deltas above over this blended total.

Model: each channel's response follows result ∝ budget^p (a standard diminishing-returns curve shape), calibrated so it reproduces your entered budget/result exactly at the current allocation, then projects forward or back as you move the slider. p is an assumed elasticity (not fitted from your historical time-series — this app has no time-series to fit from), so treat the projected deltas as directionally useful, not a guaranteed forecast. A real MMM (Meridian, Robyn) fits p per channel from months of real spend/response data.
